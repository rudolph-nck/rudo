# Core Work Hub — Quick Guide for IT Core

**Open it:** double-click the **Core Work Hub** icon on your desktop. Edge opens the hub, already signed in as you. (No icon yet? Open the shared hub folder and double-click **Install desktop icon.bat** once.)

**Service Desk is still where tickets live.** Assign, set waiting statuses, comment and close tickets there; the hub follows within 15 minutes. The hub is where we decide **what to work on, in what order, and by whom**.

## Your day
1. **Notifications** (top of the sidebar): tickets where a requester is owed a response or update. Fix the red ones first.
2. **My work**: everything you own. Acknowledge new assignments.
3. **Board → Ready to pick up**: when you have a free Doing slot, press **Pick up** on the top card.
4. After you comment on a ticket in Service Desk, the hub sees it on the next sync. Need it cleared now, or want the ready-made wording? Use **Log update** on the card.
5. Log time against the capacity buckets in **My work**.

## The communication standard (from our standard)
| | Critical (P1) | Everything else |
|---|---|---|
| First response (your name, ticket #, next-update date) | 2 business hours | 1 business day |
| Updates | every 2 business hours until resolved | at least weekly |
| Requester not responding | follow up every 2 h | every 48 h; copy their manager on the 2nd try; close as “Requester Unresponsive” after the 3rd |

If an update is missed, it goes to **you**, then to **whoever is on support** after a business day, then to **Nick**.

## Standup
- **Intake (Triage):** grade new tickets and Wrike projects (projects use the project effort scale: days to months): bucket, impact, urgency, effort (hover any number to see what it means). Shortcuts: **J/K** move, **1–4** priority, **Enter** send, **O** open in Service Desk.
- **Team score:** rate projects 1–5 (hover for meanings). Ties are settled in the meeting.
- **Priorities:** all open work P1–P4 and any conflicts.
- **Overview:** what's held up and everyone's load.

## Good to know
- **WIP limit:** Doing is capped (usually 2). Finish or park something first.
- **Taking work off your plate** (to On Hold / Ready / To Do) asks for a reason.
- **On support:** you also grade and rank during your shift; your shift stats are on **Rotation**.
- **Forced priority** (red **FORCED** tag): leadership asked for it; it goes first.
- **Time off:** see who's out and who's covering on **Time off**.
- Hover almost anything for an explanation. Everything you do is in the **Audit log**.
