# Core Work Hub — Quick Guide for IT Core

**New here?** Open **Training** in the sidebar: short lessons with a video, step-by-step how-tos, and **Show me**, a guided tour on the real screen.

**Open it:** double-click the **Core Work Hub** icon on your desktop. Edge opens the hub, already signed in as you. (No icon yet? Open the shared hub folder and double-click **Install desktop icon.bat** once.)

**Service Desk is still where tickets live.** Assign, set waiting statuses, comment and close tickets there; the hub follows within 15 minutes. The hub is where we decide **what to work on, in what order, and by whom**.

## Your day
1. **Notifications** (top of the sidebar): tickets where a requester is owed a response or update. Fix the red ones first.
2. **My work**: everything you own. Acknowledge new assignments.
3. **Board → Ready to pick up**: when you have a free Doing slot, press **Pick up** on the top card.
4. After you comment on a ticket in Service Desk, the hub sees it on the next sync. Need it cleared now, or want the ready-made wording? Use **Log update** on the card.
5. Log time against the capacity buckets in **My work**.

## The priority standard
Business hours: Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri 8:30–5:30, Thu 10:00–5:30. All times are business time.

| | What it means | First response | Updates | Target to resolve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Critical** | Core or member-facing system down / badly impaired, no workaround; regulatory or money-movement risk | 1 hour | every hour (after hours: on-call, on any change, and first thing next morning) | same business day; all hands |
| **P2 High** | One department or process significantly affected; member-facing with a workaround; hard deadline | same day (4 business hours) | daily | 3 business days |
| **P3 Medium** | One team or user affected, workaround exists | 1 business day | twice a week | 10 business days |
| **P4 Low** | Requests, questions, minor changes | 1 business day | weekly | 20 business days |

Requester not responding: follow up every 48 h (P1 every 2 h); copy their manager on the 2nd try; close as “Requester Unresponsive” after the 3rd.

**Nothing gets neglected:** past its target, a P3 or P4 is treated one level higher (two at twice the target, never P1 automatically). Work that keeps getting passed over is flagged after 3 bumps. Departments whose work hasn't been touched in 10 business days are flagged.

If an update is missed, it goes to **you**, then to **whoever is on support** after a business day, then to **Nick**.

## Stand-up
- **Stand-up page** (run by Nick and whoever is on support), in order: open P1s, **due today** (last day of target, e.g. a P2 on day 3 of 3: address it or re-prioritize), past target, missed commitments, **coming up** (next two business days, a quick touch base), repeatedly bumped, departments waiting, and the backlog to clear next.
- On each row: mark **On track / At risk / Blocked** (add a **Note** if useful), set a **Date** (commitment), or change the **Priority** (lowering needs a reason). The counter shows how many have been discussed today. **Copy for Teams** posts the list with today's check-ins.
- **Intake (Triage):** grade new tickets and Wrike projects (projects use the project effort scale: days to months): bucket, impact, urgency, effort (hover any number to see what it means). Shortcuts: **J/K** move, **1–4** priority, **Enter** send, **O** open in Service Desk.
- **Team score:** rate projects 1–5 (hover for meanings). Ties are settled in the meeting.
- **Priorities:** all open work P1–P4 and any conflicts.
- **Overview:** what's held up and everyone's load.

## Good to know
- **WIP limit:** Doing is capped (seniors 3, mid 3, juniors 2). Finish or park something first. During your **support week, projects pause**: support work comes first.
- **Taking work off your plate** (to On Hold / Ready / To Do) asks for a reason.
- **On support:** you also grade and rank during your shift; your shift stats are on **Rotation**.
- **Forced priority** (red **FORCED** tag): leadership asked for it; it goes first.
- **Time off:** see who's out and who's covering on **Time off**.
- **Your avatar:** click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to pick a colour, an emoji, or a photo (your Windows picture or an upload). The whole team sees it.
- Hover almost anything for an explanation. Everything you do is in the **Audit log**.
