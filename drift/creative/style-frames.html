The eight frames

Motion test

Two and a half seconds around WEIGHT (0:32.8–0:35.3), rendered at full quality. The letters fall under gravity with a 25 ms stagger, their shadows rush toward them from the overhead lamp, they land on the kick, bounce once (6% of the drop, damped) and settle. The camera takes the hit and recovers.

This is the motion standard for the build: heavy things obey gravity and inertia, light things obey air drag and the voice. No generic easing curves on anything that's supposed to have mass.
