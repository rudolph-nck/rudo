How it works

Every line is an object

Each lyric is typed, printed, stamped, engraved or signed onto something real from your life: a phone screen, a form, a monitor, a map, a note.

Type follows the object

Bold sans for big moments, typewriter for forms and screens, engraved serif for plaques and star charts, handwriting for Dad, notes and signatures.

One hot orange

Black and paper-white, plus one orange for the word being sung, the spark, pins and stamps. Nothing else is coloured.

The battery gauge

Like their P(doom) meter: huge thin outlined numbers that return at key moments. It drains through the verses and charges in the choruses.

Objects hand off

Each object turns into the next: a page tears, a line becomes a chart, a dot becomes a pin, a screen powers off.

Small details reward a rewatch

Timestamps, footnotes, tiny labels and UI copy, all written for Drift.

Pacing

A new object for nearly every line in verses (2–3 s), bigger and faster in choruses, long holds for the voicemail and the bridge.

The figure and the spark

You appear as a silhouette at a few moments (rooftop, driving, the X-ray), and the spark is the orange point that runs through everything.
