Rules for every section

The spark is you

One light carries through the whole video: dust in the sunbeam, the crack's first light, free in the chorus, rebuilding the shelf, last thing on screen.

Scenes turn into each other

Clock into fan, burnt-out spark into a blink, the crack into the void into the chorus sky, stars into highway lights. No hard cuts between worlds.

Heavy verses, weightless choruses

In verses gravity wins: words fall, break, weigh things down. On “I just wanna drift” gravity switches off.

Amber is the word being sung

The type stays flat and crisp. Only the spark glows; the sung word is hot amber, then cools to white.

Something new every 2–3 s

A new angle, object or scene change. The camera never parks.

Camera follows the singing

Held notes ease into slow motion; fast lines get quicker moves and whips. Every beat gives the camera a small punch.

The bass moves the world

Stars, dust, the fan, the strings, the shelf and the car all react to the kick and the bass.

It loops

The last frame matches the first: the spark igniting in the dark.

Phone-safe

Key words stay clear of the Reels/TikTok buttons on the right and the caption area at the bottom.
