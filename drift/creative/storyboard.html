RUDO · Drift · Step 2 of 5 · Storyboard

Weight, then drift

One continuous journey built from the approved words. Each verse is a heavier world, each chorus a weightless one, and every repeated word comes back changed. Nothing cuts: each section is handed to the next by a word's own effect.

Throughlines

The hot point

The film's only light. It breathes on "Yeah", draws every object, runs backwards to erase on NO, and redraws him at the end.

The stone block

Crushes (WEIGHT, 0:33) → carried (bridge, 2:48) → lifted off (breakdown, 3:16) → picked up by choice (outro, 3:52).

The stars

Every DRIFT turns letters into stars. They accumulate: chorus 2 still holds chorus 1's stars, and the last DRIFT dissolves into all of them.

The erased things

Everything drawn in the verses is something a NO can later unmake: calendar, crowd, road, mask, phone, ceiling slab, trophy wall.

Underneath

Final Chorus

drift · guilt · past · hear · noise · stronger · tonight

World
Starfield again, but the camera drifts too. Below him: guilt smoke and the past, out of reach. Then the noise, small and below him.

Stripped (4:04–4:13, no beat), then a rebuild into STRONGER. Energy 53.

Tomorrow

Outro

fighting · building · weight · tonight · drift

World
A horizon line like dawn. A tower builds. He lifts the stone block by choice. Night again, the last drift, near-silence, then the beat returns.
Camera
Wide, steady.
Pacing
Steady build, then 11 s of near-silence, then the instrumental return. Energy 50.
Hand-off
End card: the hot point redraws RUDO and the title DRIFT.
