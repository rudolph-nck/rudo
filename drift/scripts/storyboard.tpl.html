Weight, then drift

One continuous journey built from the approved words. No people on screen: the words are the characters. Each verse is a heavier world, each chorus a weightless one, and every repeated word comes back changed. Nothing cuts: each section is handed to the next by a word's own effect.

Throughlines

The hot point

The film's only light. It breathes on "Yeah", draws every object, runs backwards to erase on NO, and redraws the RUDO wordmark at the end.

The stone block

Crushes (WEIGHT, 0:33) → carried (bridge, 2:48) → lifted off (breakdown, 3:16) → picked up by choice (outro, 3:52).

The stars

Every DRIFT turns letters into stars. They accumulate: chorus 2 still holds chorus 1's stars, and the last DRIFT dissolves into all of them.

The erased things

Everything drawn in the verses is something a NO can later unmake: calendar, crowd, road, mask, phone, ceiling slab, trophy wall.
